Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5th, 2026

Joe Martinez operates the Simple Craps mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Simple Craps is a simulated gambling game. We do not collect any personal information directly through the app. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

2. Third-Party Services

The Service uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob & Meta Audience Network: Used to serve advertisements. These services may collect and use certain data, such as your device's Advertising ID, to provide personalized ads or measure ad performance.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process in-app purchases. Transactions are handled securely by Google. We do not store your credit card or payment information.
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to collect anonymous usage data and app performance metrics to help us improve the game experience. This data is not linked to your identity.

3. Simulated Gambling

Simple Craps features simulated gambling themes. No real money or items of value can be won or lost in this game. The "Bank" and "Chips" used in the game are virtual and have no real-world monetary value.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Deletion & Privacy

Simple Craps does not use user accounts and does not collect or store any personally identifiable information on its own servers. All game progress, bankroll data, and roll statistics are stored locally on your device.

If you wish to delete your data, you can do so at any time by clearing the app's storage or cache in your device settings, or by uninstalling the application. Any pseudonymous data collected by third-party services (such as Google AdMob or Firebase) is managed by those providers; you can manage your advertising preferences or reset your Advertising ID via your Android device settings.
