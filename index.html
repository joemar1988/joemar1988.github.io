Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is Simple Craps free to play?
Yes! Simple Craps is free to download and play. All core features, game modes (Classic, Crapless, Easy, Betless), analytics, and strategy guides are available for free. Optional Pro upgrades remove rewarded ad requirements.
2. How does the dice generator work? Are the rolls fair?
Yes, 100%! Every roll generates two independent 6-sided dice (values 1-6) using standard system random number generation (`Random.nextInt(1, 7)`). There is zero house weighting, artificial bias, or manipulation. On two fair dice, 7 naturally occurs 16.67% of the time (1 in 6 rolls).
3. What is Sevenless Practice Mode?
Sevenless Mode ("No 7s") suppresses 7s during practice rolls. This allows players to isolate and test complex multi-point betting setups, press systems, and long-roll scenarios without sevening out.
4. How does Crapless Craps handle Come Bets?
In Crapless Craps, there are no Come-Out craps losers (2, 3, 12). When you place a Come bet in Crapless Craps, 7 wins 1:1, while 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12 all establish as valid Come Points with true odds (up to 6:1 on 2 & 12).
5. Is my game data private?
Yes! Simple Craps requires no user accounts. All bankroll balances, custom settings, roll history, and session records stay stored 100% locally on your device.
