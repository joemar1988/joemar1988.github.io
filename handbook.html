1. Craps Essentials & Game Flow

Craps is played in rounds determined by the throw of two 6-sided dice. A complete round consists of two distinct phases:

A. The Come-Out Roll (Phase 1)

The first roll of a new round is the Come-Out Roll. The puck is marked OFF.

  • Natural 7 or 11: Pass Line bets win 1:1. Don't Pass bets lose. The round stays in Come-Out.
  • Craps (2, 3, or 12): Pass Line bets lose. Don't Pass wins 1:1 on 2 or 3 (12 pushes/ties). The round stays in Come-Out.
  • Point Numbers (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10): Establishes the Point! The puck flips to ON and moves to that point number.

B. Point Phase (Phase 2)

Once a Point is established, the shooter continues rolling until one of two events occurs:

  • Making the Point: The point number is rolled again. Pass Line bets win 1:1, Pass Odds win true odds, and the puck flips back to OFF for a new Come-Out roll.
  • Seven Out (7): A 7 is rolled before making the point. Pass Line and Place bets lose. Don't Pass wins. The round ends and resets to Come-Out.

2. Game Modes Explained

Simple Craps offers four specialized game variants so you can practice under exact casino conditions:

Classic Craps

Official casino rules with Pass, Don't Pass, Come, Don't Come, Place, Buy, Lay, Field, Hardways, and Proposition bets with full true odds support.

Crapless Craps

A popular modern variant where there are no Craps numbers on the Come-Out roll. 2, 3, 11, and 12 join 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 as valid Point numbers. 7 is the sole Come-Out winner. Come bets also establish points on 2, 3, 11, and 12 with true odds up to 6:1.

Easy Craps

A streamlined table variant where Place bets are presented in a clean single-tier grid and "Against" (Don't) side bets are disabled for fast, action-packed practice.

Betless Rolls & Sevenless Practice

  • Betless Rolls: Enable in Settings to throw dice without active bets to observe streak frequency or warm up.
  • Sevenless Practice ("No 7s"): Suppresses 7s during practice sessions so you can test how your betting systems perform during long, hot-hand rolls.

4. Popular Craps Betting Systems

A. The Three-Point Molly

A conservative, high-odds system designed to capitalize on hot shooters while minimizing house edge:

  1. Place a Pass Line bet on the Come-Out.
  2. Once a Point is set, take Max Pass Odds.
  3. Place a Come bet on the next roll. When established, take Max Come Odds.
  4. Place a second Come bet to maintain 3 active points on the table (Pass + 2 Come points).

B. The Iron Cross

A high-frequency action strategy that wins on 30 out of 36 possible dice combinations (every number except 7):

  1. Place Place 5, Place 6, and Place 8.
  2. Place a Field Bet on every roll.
  3. On any 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 11, 12 roll, the Field wins. On 5, 6, 8, the Place bet wins (collect profit and replace the Field).
